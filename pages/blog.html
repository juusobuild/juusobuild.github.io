Strain Gauges

I watched a YouTube video about strain gauges. The things I learned were:

  • Strain gauges measure resistance. Stretch → resistance increases.
  • Short gauge = more accuracy, long gauge = more practical.
  • Constantan is commonly used (gauge factor ~2.0).
  • Typical strain: 1–1000 με, resistance: 120Ω or 350Ω.
  • Wheatstone bridge is used for measurement.
  • Temperature compensation:
    • Dummy gauge method
    • Matched thermal expansion material
  • Rosette uses 3 gauges at different angles.
